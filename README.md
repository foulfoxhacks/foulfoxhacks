## About me

Hey! I'm **Sammy**, also known as **Sammy The Femboy Puppy**, **AKASammyThePuppy** and **FoulFoxHacks**: an independent developer, furry creator and community builder.

Most of my projects start with a practical problem: a tool that doesn't work reliably, two platforms that don't talk to each other, or a community that needs better infrastructure. I like finding out how things work and building the fix. I spend most of my time on:

- **Software:** apps, APIs, integrations, automation and developer tooling
- **Web:** full-stack sites, technical SEO, indexing and accessibility
- **Social VR:** Unity, VRChat worlds, creator tools and video players
- **Creators and communities:** streaming integrations, analytics, moderation and safety

## How I work

Find the real problem, research what already exists, build it, then verify it actually works before calling it done. I'd rather label something experimental than advertise a feature that hasn't been tested, and I write the docs as I go.

I'm happy to collaborate on open-source community infrastructure, VRChat and Unity tooling, streaming and social integrations, moderation and safety tools, or technical SEO.
